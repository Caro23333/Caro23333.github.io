I'm a first-year CSE Ph.D. student at University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, fortunately advised by [Prof. Mahdi Cheraghchi](https://mahdi.ch). 

My research interest lies in **theoretical computer science**, particularly in **computational complexity** as its interplay with other fields of computer science, e.g., coding theory and cryptography. 

Although my understanding is still limited, I'm fascinated by the following topics:

- **Explicit construction, pseudorandomness and derandomization**, as well as their applications;
- **Circuit complexity**, especially frontier techniques and limitations on proving lower bounds. 
- **Coding theory** and its application to complexity theory.

I completed my undergraduate study and obtained B.E. in IIIS (Yao Class) at Tsinghua University. I was appreciative for the opportunity to work under [Prof. Igor Carboni Oliveira](https://www.dcs.warwick.ac.uk/~igorcarb/) during my third year of undergraduate.
